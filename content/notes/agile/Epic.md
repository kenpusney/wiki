
An Epic is a large body of work in [[SAFe]] that spans multiple teams and Program Increments. Epics live in the Portfolio Backlog and are broken down into Features.

## What is an Epic?

An Epic is a significant initiative that delivers business value. It's too large to be completed in a single Program Increment and typically requires coordination across multiple teams.

### Epic vs Feature vs [[User Stories|User Story]]

| Aspect | Epic | Feature | [[User Stories|User Story]] |
|--------|------|---------|--------------------------|
| **Scope** | Strategic initiative | Business capability | Small, implementable piece |
| **Level** | Portfolio | Program | Team |
| **Duration** | Multiple PIs | Multiple Sprints | Single Sprint |
| **Owner** | Lean Portfolio Management | Product Management | Product Owner |
| **Estimated** | T-shirt sizing | T-shirt sizing | [[User Story Points|Story points]] |

## Epic Lifecycle

1. **Idea** — New opportunity or need identified
2. **Hypothesis** — Business case and success metrics defined
3. **Review** — Portfolio Kanban reviews the epic
4. **Approved** — Added to the Portfolio Backlog
5. **Decomposed** — Broken down into Features
6. **Implemented** — Features built over multiple PIs
7. **Measured** — Business outcomes evaluated

## Epic in [[SAFe]]

In [[SAFe]], epics are part of the portfolio-level artifacts:

- **Epic** — Portfolio level
- **Feature** — Program level
- **[[User Stories|User Story]]** — Team level

Epics flow from the Portfolio Backlog into the Program Backlog as Features.
