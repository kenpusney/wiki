
Whole Team is an [[Extreme Programming|XP]] practice where everyone involved in the project works together in the same space (physical or virtual), sharing responsibility for success.

## The Whole Team Concept

In XP, there are no separate "business" and "technical" teams. Everyone is on the same team, working toward the same goals:

- **Customers** define what to build and why
- **Developers** figure out how to build it
- **Testers** ensure quality
- **Designers** shape the user experience
- **Everyone** shares responsibility for the product

## Key Principles

### Shared space
- Co-located when possible
- If distributed, use tools that simulate co-location
- Regular face-to-face interaction

### Shared responsibility
- No "throw it over the wall" mentality
- Everyone owns the product's success
- Collective accountability

### Direct communication
- Face-to-face conversations over documents
- Quick questions, quick answers
- No waiting for formal meetings

### Customer involvement
- Customer is part of the team, not a client
- Customer is available for questions
- Customer participates in demos and planning

## Benefits of Whole Team

### Faster decisions
- No waiting for approvals from distant managers
- Decisions made by the people doing the work
- Less bureaucracy

### Better products
- Diverse perspectives improve design
- Customer feedback is immediate
- Quality is everyone's responsibility

### Higher morale
- Team members feel ownership
- Shared success builds camaraderie
- Less finger-pointing

## Challenges and Solutions

### "Our customer can't be on-site"
- Schedule regular check-ins (daily or every few days)
- Use video calls for real-time communication
- Make the customer available via chat/messaging
- Document decisions and share widely

### "We're distributed across time zones"
- Overlap core hours for synchronous collaboration
- Use async communication for non-urgent matters
- Rotate meeting times to share the burden
- Invest in collaboration tools

### "We have specialists who need focus time"
- Balance collaboration with deep work
- Pair during complex tasks, solo for straightforward ones
- Use "office hours" for questions and collaboration

## Whole Team and XP Practices

- **[[Pair Programming]]** — Pairs are the fundamental unit of collaboration
- **[[Collective Code Ownership]]** — Everyone can change any code
- **[[Planning Game]]** — Customers and developers plan together
- **[[Sustainable Pace]]** — The whole team maintains a sustainable rhythm
- **[[Coding Standards]]** — Common standards enable collaboration
