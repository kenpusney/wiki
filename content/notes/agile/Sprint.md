
A Sprint is a fixed time-box of one month or less during which a "Done", useable, and potentially releasable product Increment is created. Sprints are the heartbeat of Scrum.

## What is a Sprint?

A Sprint is a consistent duration throughout the development of the product. During the Sprint:
- No changes are made that would endanger the Sprint Goal
- Quality goals do not decrease
- The Product Backlog is refined as needed
- Scope may be clarified and re-negotiated with the Product Owner

## Sprint Length

| Sprint Length | When to Use |
|---------------|-------------|
| **1 week** | High uncertainty, need fast feedback |
| **2 weeks** | Most common, good balance |
| **3 weeks** | Complex work, longer feedback loops |
| **4 weeks** | Maximum allowed, stable requirements |

Shorter sprints provide faster feedback but require more overhead. Longer sprints allow more work but delay feedback.

## What Happens During a Sprint

### Beginning: [[Sprint Planning]]
- The team plans the work for the Sprint
- A [[Sprint Goal]] is established
- Work is selected from the [[Sprint Backlog]]

### Middle: [[Daily Scrum]]
- The team inspects progress daily
- Adjusts the plan as needed
- Identifies and removes blockers

### End: [[Sprint Review]] and [[Retrospectives]]
- The team presents the [[Increment]] to stakeholders
- The team reflects on how to improve

## Sprint Rules

1. **No changes to the Sprint Goal** — Once started, the Sprint Goal cannot be changed
2. **Quality does not decrease** — The [[Definition of Done]] must be met
3. **The Sprint can be cancelled** — But only by the Product Owner, and only if the Sprint Goal becomes obsolete
4. **Sprints are time-boxed** — They end on time, regardless of whether the work is complete

## Sprint Anti-Patterns

### "Let's extend the Sprint"
Sprints are time-boxed for a reason. Extensions delay feedback and reduce urgency.

### "Let's add more work"
Scope should not increase during a Sprint. If new work emerges, it goes to the [[Sprint Backlog]] for the next Sprint.

### "We're almost done, let's skip the Retrospective"
The [[Retrospectives|Retrospective]] is essential for improvement. Skipping it means missing learning opportunities.

### "The Sprint Goal doesn't matter"
The Sprint Goal provides focus and alignment. Without it, the team is just doing random tasks.

## Sprint and Scrum Artifacts

- **Product Backlog** — Source of work for future Sprints
- **Sprint Backlog** — Work planned for the current Sprint
- **Increment** — Result of the Sprint

## Sprint and Scrum Events

- **[[Sprint Planning]]** — Plans the Sprint
- **[[Daily Scrum]]** — Inspects progress during the Sprint
- **[[Sprint Review]]** — Inspects the Increment at the end
- **[[Retrospectives]]** — Improves the process for the next Sprint
