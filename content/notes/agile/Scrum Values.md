
The Scrum Values are five foundational values that guide the Scrum framework. They create a culture of openness, respect, and commitment that enables the Scrum practices to work effectively.

## The Five Scrum Values

### 1. Commitment
Scrum Team members **commit** to achieving their goals and to supporting each other.

- Commit to the [[Sprint Goal]]
- Commit to quality ([[Definition of Done]])
- Commit to the team's success
- Commit to continuous improvement

### 2. Focus
Everyone **focuses** on the work of the Sprint and the goals of the Scrum Team.

- Focus on the Sprint Goal
- Focus on the most important work first
- Minimize distractions and context switching
- Focus on delivering value

### 3. Openness
The Scrum Team and its stakeholders are **open** about the work and the challenges.

- Open about progress and blockers
- Open to feedback and change
- Open about mistakes and learnings
- Transparent in all work

### 4. Respect
Scrum Team members **respect** each other as competent, independent people.

- Respect different perspectives and skills
- Respect the team's self-organization
- Respect the Product Owner's decisions
- Respect the Scrum Master's guidance

### 5. Courage
Scrum Team members have the **courage** to do the right thing and work on tough problems.

- Courage to say "no"
- Courage to change direction
- Courage to admit mistakes
- Courage to challenge the status quo

## How the Values Support Scrum

The Scrum Values enable the Scrum framework to work:

| Value | Enables |
|-------|---------|
| **Commitment** | Sprint Goal, Definition of Done |
| **Focus** | Sprint Backlog, Daily Scrum |
| **Openness** | Sprint Review, Retrospectives |
| **Respect** | Self-organization, Cross-functional teams |
| **Courage** | Adaptation, Transparency |

## Living the Values

### In Sprint Planning
- **Commitment**: Commit to a realistic Sprint Goal
- **Focus**: Prioritize the most important work
- **Openness**: Discuss concerns and risks
- **Respect**: Listen to all perspectives
- **Courage**: Push back on unrealistic demands

### In Daily Scrum
- **Commitment**: Update progress toward the Sprint Goal
- **Focus**: Keep the meeting focused and short
- **Openness**: Raise blockers honestly
- **Respect**: Support teammates who are stuck
- **Courage**: Admit when you need help

### In Sprint Review
- **Commitment**: Show what was actually completed
- **Focus**: Demonstrate the Increment
- **Openness**: Welcome stakeholder feedback
- **Respect**: Listen to all feedback
- **Courage**: Accept difficult feedback

### In Retrospectives
- **Commitment**: Commit to improvement actions
- **Focus**: Discuss the most important issues
- **Openness**: Share honest feedback
- **Respect**: Listen without blame
- **Courage**: Raise uncomfortable topics
